# 09 — The last capstone, and what was done about the ending

**What to build:** Movement 4's capstone and the book capstone, which is where the ending gets
handled.

The book capstone has three parts.

It names the manuscript facts plainly: the two oldest complete copies stop at 16:8, the twelve
verses printed after it are a later addition, some manuscripts carry a different shorter ending, a
few carry both, and five verse numbers elsewhere in this book are empty for the same reason. This
is the `tensions` entry, and it is the most literal case of the canon arguing with itself anywhere
in the app.

It names what the book does with its own hero: nobody inside it sees him, his family thinks he has
lost his mind, his followers fail at every turn, and the only human who says it plainly is the
officer who killed him.

And it closes on what the stop is for. The book ends before anybody succeeds, which leaves the
telling with whoever is reading it.

When this ticket closes, the book is complete and publication becomes a live decision, as does the
free sample.
