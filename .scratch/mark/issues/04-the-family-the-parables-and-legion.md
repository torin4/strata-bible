# 04 — Author Mark 3:7 to 5:43, the family, the parables and Legion

**What to build:** Three sittings, and the chapter this app was built to handle.

`mark-3` is 3:7–35: the twelve appointed, his family coming to take charge of him because people
are saying he is out of his mind, the Beelzebul accusation, and his answer about who his mother and
brothers are. Mark's portrait of the family is the harshest in the four gospels and it is not
softened here.

`mark-4` is the parables and the storm. The reading has to hold the hardest sentence in the
chapter, which is the reason he gives for speaking in parables: so that they may be ever seeing
but never perceiving. That is quoted from Isaiah 6 and it says the opposite of what most readers
expect a parable to be for.

`mark-5` is Legion, the woman with the flow of blood, and Jairus's daughter. Legion is where the
history layer earns the whole app: the name is a Roman military unit of several thousand, the pigs
are unclean and the emblem of the legion stationed in the region, and the herd drowns in the sea,
which is what the people living under occupation wanted done to the occupiers. Say it plainly and
let the reader do the arithmetic.
