# 05 — Author Mark 6 to 8:21, and close the first movement

**What to build:** Four sittings and the movement capstone.

`mark-6` is Nazareth, the mission of the twelve, the death of John told as a flashback at a
birthday party, the five thousand, and the walking on the water. The Nazareth scene carries the
sentence the later gospels edit: he could not do any miracles there. Matthew changes it to did not
do many.

`mark-7` is what defiles a person, the Syrophoenician woman, and Ephphatha. The Syrophoenician
woman is the only person in the book who wins an argument with him, and the reading does not
rescue him from the exchange.

`mark-8a` is the four thousand, the demand for a sign, the leaven, and the question that ends the
movement: do you still not understand? That question is addressed to the disciples and, by the
construction of the movement, to the reader.

The movement capstone closes on the question rather than answering it, and does not pre-empt what
movement 2 does with the two blind men.
