# 03 — The priest, and the craftsman

**What to build:** Exodus 28 to 31, selected: who wears what, and who makes it.

The priest's clothing is specified in more detail than most of the furniture, and the detail that
matters is what he carries. The names of the twelve tribes are cut into stones on his shoulders
and worn over his heart, so that he goes into the presence carrying the people on his body. That
is the reading's centre.

Then Bezalel. God names a craftsman and says he has filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill
and ability and knowledge in all kinds of crafts. It is the first time in the Bible anyone is said
to be filled with God's Spirit, and it is not a prophet or a priest or a king. It is a man who
works metal, stone and wood. A reading that has just spent chapters on specifications is the right
place to notice that the book thinks making things is a spiritual office.

The stretch closes with the sabbath restated, which is worth keeping: even the building of the
sanctuary stops on the seventh day.

One passage per chapter, since the reading spans several.
