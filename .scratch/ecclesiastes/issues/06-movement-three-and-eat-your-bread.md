# 06 — Movement 3, and the sentence the book is for

**What to build:** Movement 3 declared, movement 2's capstone and doorway, and two sittings.

`ecc-9` is where the book turns. It opens on the one fate that comes to everyone, says that the dead
know nothing, and then, in the same breath, says go, eat your bread with joy and drink your wine
with a glad heart, wear white, enjoy life with the one you love, and do your work with your might.
That sequence is the whole argument of the book compressed: the permission is granted on the basis
of the bleakness, not in spite of it. It also carries the tension on the dead knowing nothing.

`ecc-10` is the second `saying-cluster`, the wisdom-and-folly sayings, and it is the lighter of the
two: a dead fly in the perfume, a snake that bites before it is charmed, a bird carrying your words.
