# 04 — Movement 2, and Ecclesiastes 5 to 6

**What to build:** Movement 2 declared with its situation panel, movement 1's doorway, and two
sittings.

`ecc-5` runs from how to behave in a place of worship, which is mostly an instruction to say less,
through the officials watching officials, to money that never satisfies, and ends on your portion.

`ecc-6` is the shortest chapter and the bleakest: a man given everything who cannot enjoy it, and
the judgement that a stillborn child is better off. It should not be softened, and it should not be
left as the last word either, because the book itself does not leave it there.

The situation panel is where the book's date and setting go: the Persian-period Hebrew, the
economic world of absentee owners and layered officials that chapter 5 describes, and why a book
this sceptical is in the canon at all.
