# 10 — Movement capstone

**What to build:** The look-back that closes the movement. A reader who finishes the Song reaches
it, and it carries the meaning of the whole stretch, which no single reading holds on its own.

Its work is to return to the cost. The movement has been a deliverance, and it has been paid for
by dead children and a drowned army, and the reader has been asked to sing about it. The capstone
holds those together and declines to resolve them. It carries the internal tensions that only
surface at the level of the whole, which is what the capstone's tension field exists for.

It closes the movement and nothing more. There is no doorway, because the next movement does not
exist yet, and the content validator fails a doorway pointing at a movement that is not there.
The doorway arrives with the movement it points to.

It must not read as a book-level wrap-up. Exodus is one movement of four so far, and the reader
should be left at the far shore of the sea with the road to the mountain still ahead, not handed
a conclusion the book has not reached.

When this ticket is done, movement 1 is complete: eleven readings, seven sittings, a situation
panel, a composition panel, and a capstone, all unpublished and all green.
