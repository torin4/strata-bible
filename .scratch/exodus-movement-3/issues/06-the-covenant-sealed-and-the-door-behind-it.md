# 06 — The covenant sealed, and the door behind it

**What to build:** The ceremony that closes the covenant, the look-back over the whole movement,
and the doorway movement 2 has been waiting for.

Exodus 24 as a scene. Moses reads the terms aloud, the people answer that they will do everything
the LORD has said, and blood is thrown against the altar and then over the people themselves.
Then seventy elders go up the mountain, see the God of Israel with something like a pavement of
sapphire under his feet, and eat and drink. It is the strangest and most physical thing in the
movement, and it ends the code by turning it back into an event.

The capstone holds the movement whole. Its work is to say what a covenant is, that the law arrived
inside a relationship rather than as a standalone rulebook, and to return to what the code
contains without settling it. The honest position is the one this movement has been building
toward: a document that both restrains a master more than its neighbours did and permits a person
to be owned, kept by a people who went on revising it in their own scriptures.

Movement 2 gains its doorway, pointing at movement 3, which it could not have while movement 3 did
not exist. Movement 3 gains none, because movement 4 does not exist either. That chain is the same
one movements 1 and 2 have already been through.

## Comments

**Exodus 24 in two scenes.** The terms read aloud and agreed a third time, then blood from the same
animals thrown against the altar and over the people, which puts both parties in the same substance
and leaves nobody able to say afterward that they had not really entered it. Then seventy-four men
see the God of Israel, the narrator describes only what was under his feet, and they eat.

**The capstone says what a covenant is and then refuses to tidy what it contains.** Terms arriving
inside a relationship that already exists, after a rescue that already happened. Nothing here is
the price of being freed. And in the same document, a cap on how long a man may be owned beside the
assumption that he may be; a master liable for a servant's eye beside a master unpunished for a
beating; a life priced at thirty shekels a few chapters from the command not to oppress a foreigner
because you were one.

**Its tension is the movement-level one.** That the law carries divine authority as it stands,
against a canon that revises the release, condemns a nation for revoking one, and concedes
elsewhere that a provision was written in for hardness of heart. Left standing.

**The doorway chain is now complete.** Movement 1 to 2, movement 2 to 3, and movement 3 pointing
nowhere because movement 4 does not exist. The test now walks every movement but the last and
checks its target resolves, rather than asserting one hardcoded edge.

**The capstone runs 277 words** against the Genesis range of 200 to 257. Slightly long, and left
that way: it is holding three things at once and the third paragraph is the one that keeps the
movement from reading as either an endorsement or a demolition.
