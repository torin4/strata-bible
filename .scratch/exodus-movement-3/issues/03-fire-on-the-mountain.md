# 03 — Fire on the mountain

**What to build:** Exodus 19, the scene the whole covenant comes out of.

Three months out of Egypt the people camp in front of a mountain, and before a single law is
given they are offered terms and agree to them without having heard what they are. Then the
mountain is fenced, the people are told not to touch it on pain of death, and it goes dark with
smoke and shakes.

This is the familiar render path, a narrative scene, and it carries no new mechanical risk. Its
work is to put the law in a setting: whatever comes next in this movement arrives out of an
encounter rather than out of a filing cabinet, and it arrives on terms that were accepted in
advance.

The turn names something already true of the reader rather than making a demand, keeping the
register the first two movements established.
