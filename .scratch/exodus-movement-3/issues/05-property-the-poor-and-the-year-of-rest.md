# 05 — Property, the poor, and the year of rest

**What to build:** The rest of the Covenant Code, where the law turns outward.

Exodus 22 and 23, selected. Restitution and property, then a run of laws that are the reason this
code matters: do not mistreat a foreigner, because you were foreigners in Egypt. Do not exploit a
widow or an orphan. If you take a cloak in pledge, return it before sunset, because it is the only
covering they have. Lend without interest to the poor among you. Do not follow a crowd into
wrongdoing. Let the land rest in the seventh year so the poor may eat from it.

Most of these are marked as still claiming the reader, and that is the point of placing this
reading directly after the servant laws. The same code that permits a person to be owned also
grounds its treatment of the outsider in the memory of having been one. Both are Exodus. A reader
who has just been shown the first should meet the second in the same voice.

**One passage per chapter, without exception.** This reading spans two chapters, and a single
passage running from chapter 22 into chapter 23 would have verse numbers that do not ascend. The
verse-integrity check attributes a bare number to a chapter only when the numbers ascend, so it
would mark the passage unverifiable and skip it in silence. The reading would appear to carry the
BSB guarantee while not actually having it. Two passages, one per chapter, and the guarantee
holds. The plague reading solved the same problem the same way.
