# 05 — Author Ruth 4, the gate and the genealogy

**What to build:** The last sitting. A property transaction, a child, and a list of names.

Ruth 4 is a legal scene: ten elders at the town gate, a nearer redeemer who is willing to buy the
land until he learns what comes with it, a sandal changing hands, and a marriage settled in
public. The ground note carries how such a transaction worked and why the gate is where it
happened.

Three things the reading has to get right.

The women's blessing over Naomi says the child will renew her life and sustain her old age. That
is a grandchild, and it is not her husband and it is not her sons. The book does not give back
what it took, and the reading says so plainly.

The child is named by the neighbour women, not by his parents, and they say a son has been born
to Naomi. The book's last image of her is a woman with a baby on her lap who is not hers.

The genealogy makes a Moabite the great-grandmother of David, in the last line, on purpose. Nobody
inside the story knows it. That is the closing note, and it belongs to the book capstone as much
as to this reading.

## Comments

**Nobody consults Ruth in the scene that decides her life.** She is discussed by name, in public, as
an item attached to a field, by men settling an inheritance. The reading says so rather than
working around it. What Boaz does inside that machinery is refuse the loophole the nearer man
takes, and the nearer man, in a book that names everybody, is never given a name.

**The misreading is the romance.** The book never says he loved her. It says he is a redeemer, that
he did what the nearer man would not, and that the business on the table was a dead man's field and
a dead man's name. A man who took on a duty that cost him is a more useful thing to be shown than a
man who felt something.

**What is not given back is stated plainly**, in the scene where the women hand Naomi the baby. The
child will renew her life and sustain her old age, and both are true, and neither is her husband or
her sons.

**The genealogy scene was trimmed twice.** Sixty-two words of scripture cannot carry much
apparatus, and it came in at 2.5x. Cutting it to a ground line, the arithmetic on the fourth name
from the end, one turn and one ask brought it to 1.6x, and the scene reads better spare. A list of
names should not be buried in commentary.
